1. Data We Collect

To provide this service, we collect the following personal data:

  • Account information: username, email address, an encrypted password hash, and avatar image;
  • Usage data: IP address, login time, and activity logs (posts, replies, likes, and more);
  • Authentication data: a login cookie used to maintain your session.

We do not collect sensitive identity information such as your real name, national ID, or phone number (unless you actively provide it in your profile).

2. How We Use Data

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Provide core features such as the forum, video, and live streaming;
  • Protect account security and prevent abuse (such as cleaning up unverified emails or banning violators);
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to rightsholder complaints.

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Data is stored on servers located in Hong Kong. Database files are kept on the server, and passwords are stored as irreversible hashes. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

4. Live Streaming Data

Live streaming uses peer-to-peer (P2P) technology. Audio and video data goes directly from the broadcaster's device to viewers' devices, and never passes through or is stored on our servers.

We only forward the minimal signaling data needed to establish a connection (such as a small amount of text like usernames). We do not touch, record, or store live audio/video content itself. Transmission stops when the stream ends, and no recording is retained.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use a login cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or perform cross-site behavioral analysis. You can clear cookies in your browser settings, although this may require you to log in again.

6. Third-Party Services

This service relies on the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Cloudflare: content delivery and security protection, which may process your IP address;
  • Google STUN: a public STUN server used to establish live connections for NAT traversal.

We do not proactively share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law or in response to a valid rightsholder complaint.

7. Your Content

Posts and submissions you publish in the forum and video sections are stored on this service for display. When you delete your account, related content is removed as well.

Due to the P2P architecture, live-streaming content is not stored on servers; see Section 4.

8. Your Rights

Under the Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Right to correction: request correction of inaccurate or outdated data;
  • Right to deletion: request deletion of your account and related data.

To exercise these rights, contact us via the operating email address. We will respond within a reasonable period.

9. Children

This service is not directed at children under the age of 13. If we learn that a child has created an account without parental or guardian consent, we will promptly delete the related information.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this service, and continued use of the service indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For any questions about this policy or the protection of your personal data, please contact us via the operating email address.
